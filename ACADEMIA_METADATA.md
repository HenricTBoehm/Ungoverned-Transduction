**Title:** Ungoverned Transduction: When AI-Enabled Effects Outrun Authority

**Author:** Henric T. Böhm

**Version:** 0.3.1 — 27 October 2026

**Document type:** Independent Concept Note / non-peer-reviewed discussion preprint.

**Keywords:** AI governance; ungoverned transduction; PADC; output-to-action admissibility; authority promotion; workflow authorization; Shadow AI; accountability; auditability.

**Proposed abstract:** AI capability may fail to yield an observable organizational effect or may reach effect without adequately warranted execution authority. This concept note separates observed effect, transition admissibility and value realization. It specifies a three-valued classification and illustrates it through a synthetic procurement-status counterexample and eight deterministic test cases. A local permission check can pass when end-to-end admissibility fails, but a strong conventional authorization baseline detects the same stipulated failure. The demonstration is model-relative, and neither empirical prevalence nor incremental performance of PADC relative to competent enterprise controls is established. The note identifies explicit reduction and defeat conditions for subsequent comparative testing.
